# Butgetbath
Lead-generation website for Budget Bath Solutions (bathroom remodeling and tile in Kane, Kendall, DuPage and DeKalb counties, Illinois).

- Static, SEO-focused site generated by `build.js` (Bootstrap 5.3), with homeowner help, community Q&A, service-area and legal pages.
- Free customer accounts: email verification, photo and video uploads (photos up to 8 MB, MP4/MOV/WebM videos up to 100 MB, 10 files per request) with per-file comments, and a threaded request (ticket) system with categories, priorities, a progress stepper, upcoming appointments, a staff inbox (search, sort, filters), internal notes, email notifications and email replies. Email is sent with Resend.
- Landing page built for booking: an above-the-fold estimate form (preferred day and time), FAQ with schema markup and local business schema.

Requires Node 24 or newer. From a clean checkout:

```
npm ci
npm run build
npm start    # builds and serves on http://localhost:3000
npm test
```

The lockfile is authoritative; use `npm ci` for reproducible local and CI installs. Configuration, Resend setup, backup/restore, monitoring and deployment notes are in [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md). Copy `.env.example` to `.env`, configure only the integrations you use, and never commit it.

## Appointments

Staff (`ADMIN_EMAILS`) schedule, reschedule and cancel appointments from a customer's request page. Each change emails the customer a confirmation with an `.ics` invite (`METHOD:REQUEST`/`CANCEL`), plus "Add to Google Calendar" and Apple/Outlook links. Customers see upcoming appointments on their dashboard and can cancel (staff are emailed).

- **iCalendar**: per-appointment download at `/api/appointments/:id/ics`, and a private subscription feed for staff (shown on the staff requests page) that works with Apple Calendar, Outlook and Google Calendar "From URL".
- **Google Calendar sync** (optional): set `GOOGLE_CALENDAR_ID` and `GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON` (see `.env.example`). Without them, only the links, `.ics` files and feed are used.

## Online booking (customers)

Customers book without an account at `/book/` (also the home page hero, the "Free Estimate" buttons and the thank-you page): choose a **phone call (30 min), video call (30 min) or free in-home estimate (90 min)**, pick an open day and time, and enter name, phone, email (plus the address for a visit). Only real open times are offered: weekday hours, minimum notice, a travel buffer after in-home visits and a per-slot capacity, all editable under **Admin > Bookings > Availability** (closed days too). Staff-scheduled in-home appointments also block time. Booking is race-safe: the slot is re-checked when saving.

- **Calendar**: the confirmation email attaches an `.ics` invite (alarms one day and one hour before) and links to Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar. The success screen has the same buttons.
- **Reminders**: an email goes out about 24 hours before (checked every 5 minutes).
- **Manage**: the confirmation has a private link (`/book/manage/?token=...`) to reschedule or cancel; changes send updated or cancel invites and alert the office. Signed-in customers see their bookings on the account dashboard. The link is an HMAC of the booking id (`booking_secret` setting).
- **Staff**: Admin > Bookings lists upcoming and past bookings with reschedule, cancel, done, no-show and notes; the office gets an email with an `.ics`; bookings join the staff calendar feed and Google Calendar sync. Each booking also creates or updates a customer lead (marketing stays off unless the box is ticked) and a contact entry.
- **Config**: `BUSINESS_TIMEZONE` (default `America/Chicago`) sets the hours' time zone. Set `BUSINESS_PHONE` (or `phone` in `site.json`) so the call buttons, footer and emails show your number; it is empty until you do.
- API: `GET /api/book/{config,days,slots,manage,ics}`, `POST /api/book`, `/api/book/{cancel,reschedule}`, `GET /api/account/bookings`, `/api/admin/bookings[/config|/:id/{cancel,reschedule,status,note}]`.

## Bathroom estimator
- `/estimator/` (staff, mobile-first): quick estimator, price book (parts, labor, equipment, subs), markup, contingency, tax on materials, deposits, optional add-ons, change orders, payments and legal documents. Admins can allow individual employees from their employee page; employees see only their own drafts and never markup or profit.
- Customers review, sign and pay from `/account/estimate/?id=N`.
- **Smart takeoff (`lib/bathroom.js`):** about 250 price-book items (fans, trim, flooring, paint, toilets and internals, plumbing, electrical, vanities, hardware, shower doors, glass, accessories, permits). The wizard adapts to bathroom type, size, ceiling height, home age (lead-safe, asbestos test, old supply and cast-iron drains), floor level, hidden-damage condition, flooring, shower build, glass, vanity and toilet choices, and lists suggested upgrades as optional lines. `POST /api/estimator/advice` previews notes and price live.
- **Full remodel mode:** the `fullRemodel` option guts walls and ceiling to the studs and joists and the floor to the subfloor, then adds stud inspection, full plumbing and electrical rough-in, insulation, drywall, extra haul-away and cleanup. **Results PDF:** `POST /api/estimator/advice/pdf` (staff) downloads the wizard results (notes, sections, optional upgrades, totals) from the New estimate page.
- **AI learning (`lib/learning.js`):** after a job, an admin records the real cost on the signed estimate ("Teach the AI estimator"; `POST /api/estimator/estimates/:id/outcome`). The estimator compares it with its uncalibrated prediction and learns a correction per section and from the most similar past jobs (recency-weighted, shrunk toward 1 until there is evidence, capped at -25%/+40%, so corrections never compound). Admin → **AI learning** shows what it learned and its accuracy. New estimates also get a recommended contingency (older home, poor condition, full gut) and a trade-by-trade schedule with working days.
- **Photo estimating (`lib/vision.js`):** the New estimate page can read 1 to 4 bathroom photos with a vision AI model, fill in the questions and list risks and assumptions. It is off until `AI_API_KEY` is set (optional `AI_MODEL`, default `gpt-4.1`, and `AI_BASE_URL` for any OpenAI-compatible API). Staff must confirm customer consent, photos are sent only for analysis and never stored, and only whitelisted answers are accepted. Treat results as a starting point and measure on site.
- **Crew, hours and work calendar:** every estimate gets a phase-by-phase plan with crew size, labor hours, working days and dates. Admin → Defaults sets the work day (start/end), unpaid lunch, paid breaks and whether Saturdays are worked (Sundays are always off). Productive hours per person = shift minus lunch and breaks; shorter productive days stretch the schedule, waiting phases (inspection, cure, lab) run on calendar days, and an optional planned start date drives the dates. Real job hours recorded under "Teach the AI estimator" correct future hour estimates.
- **Labor times and process playbook (`lib/labor-times.js`, `lib/playbook.js`):** crew hours are built from a labor-time library reconciled from published production rates (Craftsman estimator figures, PHCC plumbing hours, NECA labor units, tile, painting, flooring, roofing and carpentry guides; each entry names its source, see `GET /api/estimator/labor-times`) and adjusted for small-job remodel conditions. Every working phase carries an 8% cushion (`CUSHION`) for human error and unforeseen conditions; it is billed on handyman labor, shown in the schedule, and kept out of AI hour learning (`hoursBase`). Every estimate returns a `process`: the bathroom remodel in 15 ordered steps (protect, test, demo, framing, rough-in, inspection, drywall, waterproofing, tile, paint, trim-out, glass, punch list) with how-to steps, tools, supplies, this job's material quantities, crew and hours, and every handyman task has its own method (tasks you add yourself fall back to a category method). It is shown under the wizard and printed in the results PDF. The figures come from public guides, not RSMeans or other paid databases; check them against your own job records and edit task hours under Handyman tasks (edited rows are never overwritten).
- **Tools checklist:** every estimate produces a list of the tools, rentals, safety gear and supplies the job needs, built from what the estimate includes (a gut remodel adds demolition, rough-in and respirator gear; an LVP floor swaps in LVP tools). It appears on the estimate editor and the results PDF. When the job is created from a signed estimate it is copied to the employee portal, where assigned employees tick items off. Admin can refresh the crew list from the estimate. The list is built into `lib/tools.js` from standard trade practice, not from live web research; edit that file to change it.
- **Estimator insights (35 checks, `lib/insights.js`):** confidence score and price range, good/better/best, risk register with dollar exposure, margin, price per sq ft benchmark, sensitivity, price age, quote validity, shopping list, lead times, payment milestones, value engineering, aging in place, code checks, fan sizing, circuit load, tile order, dumpster size, trade hours, downtime, homeowner prep, warranty, resale, cost drivers, missing-input prompts, plain-English summary, inspections, seasonal note, premiums, allowances, learning status, cost by kind and cost per day. Admin → Defaults adds a local labor rate factor and Saturday premium. They appear under the wizard and in the results PDF. Benchmarks are reference values, not live data.
- **Estimator advisor (22 checks, `lib/advisor.js`):** scope-conflict checker, readiness by section, hidden-condition allowance, permit timeline and fee, order-by dates, price volatility and quote validity, financing examples, walk-away price, negotiation room, true profit after overhead, labor/material mix, cost of a delay day, season and holiday risks, no-bathroom plan, condo/HOA checklist, inspection calendar, dated payments, maintenance calendar, trade windows, contingency adequacy, pre-priced change-order rates and water/energy savings. The wizard also saves and resumes drafts, warns on odd inputs, explains each question, compares pinned scenarios, copies a customer summary and prints. Settings: `overhead_pct`, `target_margin_pct`, `min_margin_pct`, `finance_apr`, `water_cost_per_1000gal`, `kwh_cost` (Admin → Defaults). Reference figures are listed in `ONLINE-VERIFICATION-TODO.md`.
- **Permits and codes (`lib/codes.js`):** enter the job's city in the wizard and the estimator lists the Illinois statewide rules, which permits the scope likely needs (building, plumbing, electrical), the inspections to book, lead/asbestos/condo flags and city notes. It appears in the wizard and the results PDF. Illinois has no single statewide building code and each city adopts its own, so city entries are starter data marked unverified until an admin confirms them with the building department and saves them with `POST /api/estimator/codes`. It is guidance, not legal advice, and does not cover every Illinois municipality.
- **Handyman jobs (`lib/handyman.js`):** choose "Handyman / home repair" in New estimate and pick from about 160 tasks in 15 categories (tile & stone, drywall, carpentry, doors and windows, flooring, kitchen, plumbing, electrical, smart home, exterior, deck and fence, painting, assembly, safety and HVAC, haul-away). Each task has hours and a Home Depot reference material price. The planner adds labor at the handyman or licensed rate, a minimum service visit, permit allowances, a crew schedule, permits and inspections, lead/asbestos flags, insights and a task-based tool checklist. Admins edit task prices under Handyman tasks and rates under Defaults. Prices are reference values, not live. Handyman jobs do not feed the bathroom AI learning.
- **Downtown Chicago high-rise SEO (`build.js`):** a `/chicago-high-rise-remodeling/` hub plus pages for the Loop, River North, Streeterville, South Loop, West Loop, Gold Coast and New Eastside/Lakeshore East. Each has FAQ and Service structured data, appears in the sitemap, the nav, the footer and the home page, and Chicago is added to the business `areaServed`. Add a neighborhood by editing `hoods` in `build.js`.
- **Local prices and tool rental:** defaults are Home Depot reference prices for Kane, Kendall, DuPage and DeKalb counties (Midwest), dated in `prices_updated`. A "Tool rental" section sizes rentals (tile saw, demo hammer, mixer, air scrubber, scaffold, dolly, delivery) to the job. Items marked "(est.)" are typical local rental-yard rates. Prices cannot auto-refresh from Home Depot, so update them in the Price book; new seed items are added to existing databases at startup without overwriting edits.
- **PDFs and e-signatures:** estimates and legal documents download as PDFs (`/api/account/estimates/:id/pdf`, `/api/estimator/estimates/:id/pdf`). Typed-name signing records the name, UTC time, IP address, device, ESIGN/UETA consent text and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the signed content; the PDF prints this block, and a signed copy is emailed to the customer and `NOTIFY_EMAIL`. Admins can look up a fingerprint at `GET /api/estimator/verify?hash=...`. Signed estimates are locked; use a change order. Legal templates are generic starting points; have an attorney review them.

- **Logistics, travel and crew roles:** the wizard has an "Access, supplies, debris, cleanup and travel" card (stairs, carry distance, elevator, parking, own/rented truck or supplier delivery, dumpster/haul/junk/customer debris, who cleans, drive miles/time, tolls, site visits). `lib/logistics.js` prices every item from the price book (section "Logistics & disposal", editable under Price book) and adds the carry, supply-run and drive hours to the crew schedule. The results screen and PDF list who does what (`lib/crew-roles.js`), the day-by-day plan and the daily/final cleanup, disposal and paperwork closeout.
- **Crew job plan:** sending the tool list (or creating the job) from an estimate also saves a price-free brief in `job_briefs`. Assigned employees read it on their job under "Job plan: what each person does" (`GET /api/employee/jobs/:id/brief`).
- **Field data for the estimator:** AI learning shows daily check-in counts, delays, blockers, problems and liability records by type (`field` in `GET /api/estimator/learning`).

## Field reports and liability records

Assigned employees open **Jobs → Daily check-in, photos and liability** to:

- File a daily check-in (status, percent complete, what was done, what is left, problems, materials needed). One per day; resubmitting updates it. Problems and delays email the office.
- Upload photos and videos at every stage (JPG, PNG, WebP, MP4, MOV, WebM; content-checked).
- Record liabilities (existing damage, hidden conditions, scope changes, safety). If the homeowner agrees, a consent video and an on-screen signature are required; a refusal is recorded and alerts the office to stop work on that item.

Admins see all of it on the job page (Admin → Jobs), can review liability records, and list unreviewed ones at `/api/admin/liabilities`. Files are stored in `uploads/` and served only to assigned employees and admins. Records are append-only for employees.

## Accounting, taxes and legal paperwork

**Admin → Accounting** keeps the books and prepares everything an accountant or the IRS will ask for (cash basis, per tax year):

- Income comes from customer payments recorded on estimates, with sales tax separated out. Expenses (with receipts, mileage at your rate, job tagging and Schedule C line mapping), worker payments, and per-job profit are all tracked.
- Profit and loss, estimated self-employment and income tax, a quarterly set-aside amount, and a tax calendar.
- Workers are marked employee (W-2) or contractor (1099). The system flags missing W-4, I-9 and W-9 forms and 1099-NEC requirements ($2,000 from 2026, $600 before). Only the last four digits of a tax ID are stored.
- A legal and compliance register (licenses, insurance, bonds, EPA lead-safe, registrations) with expiry warnings, plus a required-documents checklist.
- Exports: an **accountant packet ZIP** (summary PDF, income, expenses, mileage log, payroll, W-2 and 1099 worksheets, jobs, P&L, general journal, legal register, signed agreements and liability records) or any single CSV.

It organizes and prepares the numbers; it does not file returns or calculate payroll withholding, and it is not tax or legal advice. Confirm filings with a CPA.

## Company dashboard, visitor heatmaps and holiday sales

- **Admin > Company**: 12-month charts for payments, expenses plus approved labor, leads, new customers, jobs finished and crew hours; estimate pipeline and win rate; amounts still owed; top customers; where leads and money come from.
- **Admin > Visitors**: weekday-by-hour heatmap and pages-by-weekday heatmap (in `BUSINESS_TZ`, default `America/Chicago`), landing pages, devices, browsers, operating systems and a list of recent anonymous visits. Visitors use a daily rotating hash and no IP is stored, so returning visitors and exact locations are not tracked.
- **Admin > Holiday sales**: automatic greetings for New Year's, Valentine's, Easter, Mother's/Father's Day, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Hanukkah and Christmas. Every email opens with the matching greeting (for example "Merry Christmas") and sale holidays add a discount code such as `BLACKFRIDAY26`. Each holiday can be turned on/off, given a discount, send lead time, validity and custom text, previewed, tested, or sent immediately. A scheduler checks every 30 minutes and sends once per holiday per year between 9am and 8pm.
- Only customers with an email who have not unsubscribed receive these emails. Each message has a one-click unsubscribe link and `List-Unsubscribe` headers. Set the mailing address in the Holiday sales page (CAN-SPAM). Set `HOLIDAY_AUTOMATION=off` to disable the scheduler and `HOLIDAY_MAIL_PAUSE_MS` to change the delay between sends (default 600).
- Codes are checked in the Holiday sales page; enter the discount on the customer's estimate.

## Zoho Books plugin

Admin > Money > Zoho Books connects the site to your Zoho Books organization over OAuth 2.0 (`ZohoBooks.fullaccess.all`).

1. In the Zoho API console (`api-console.zoho.<your data center>`) create a **Self Client** (paste its grant code in the admin page) or a **Server-based Application** with redirect URI `<SITE_URL>/api/admin/zoho/callback`.
2. Save the client ID, secret and data center (or set `ZOHO_CLIENT_ID`, `ZOHO_CLIENT_SECRET`, `ZOHO_DC`), connect, and pick the organization.
3. Use **Load my accounts** to copy the default expense, paid-through and deposit account IDs into the settings.

What syncs (one way to Zoho, idempotent through the `zoho_links` table, nothing is deleted in Zoho):
customers to contacts, accepted estimates to invoices (re-pushed when they change), estimate payments to customer payments applied to the invoice, expenses and contractor payments to expenses (vendors are created as contacts; the category is matched to a Zoho expense account by name, else the default). Reports (profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance) and the chart of accounts are pulled live. Auto-sync runs every `ZOHO_SYNC_MINUTES` (minimum 5) when enabled; each run is capped at 90 calls to stay under Zoho's rate limit, and per-minute 429 or expired-token responses are retried (Zoho allows 100 calls a minute per organization and 1,000 to 10,000 a day by plan; a daily-cap response stops the run until tomorrow). Failures appear in the sync log. The client secret and refresh token are never sent to the browser.

The plugin is off by default. Use **Turn on** / **Turn plugin off** on the Zoho Books page; while off, no sync (manual or automatic) or Zoho call is made, and your connection and links are kept.

## Website leads and mailing list

Every contact or estimate-form submission is saved as a contact and also creates (or updates) a customer record with status `lead` and a `website-<type>` source, so nobody is lost. Repeat submissions from the same email update that customer instead of duplicating it. The forms have an optional "send me offers" checkbox: only people who tick it (or whom the admin adds) are on the mailing list that the holiday emails use; everyone else starts opted out. In Admin > Customers you can filter by leads, website forms or the mailing list, toggle a customer's mailing status, and export the opted-in list as CSV (`/api/admin/customers/mailing-list.csv`). ## Automatic SEO files

`sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and `llms.txt` are produced by `lib/seo.js` during every build. While the server runs, `lib/seo-sync.js` watches `content.js`, `seo-content.js`, `site.json`, `gallery.json`, `build.js` and `assets/`, then rebuilds the site (into `dist.next/`, swapped in atomically) after a short pause. Sitemap `lastmod` dates only change for pages whose content really changed (state in `data/seo-state.json`). ## Security hardening

- **Sign-in:** 8 failed attempts per email in 15 minutes lock the account (also for unknown emails, so lockouts reveal nothing); sessions are capped at 10 per user and expired ones are purged; cross-site requests are refused via `Origin` and `Sec-Fetch-Site` checks.
- **GPS clock integrity** (`lib/tracking.js`): fixes that imply impossible speed are dropped, frozen/perfect-accuracy GPS (typical fake-GPS apps) is flagged, pings must come from the device that clocked in, one phone cannot be clocked in for two employees, and new devices are flagged. Findings appear on the shift timeline, as admin alerts and in the security log. These are server-side heuristics; a browser cannot prove a position is real, so review flagged shifts before paying.
- **Security log** (`lib/security.js`, Admin > Admin > Security log): sign-in failures, GPS findings and admin overrides.
- **Headers:** CSP adds `object-src 'none'`, worker and manifest scopes; request and header timeouts are set.

## Unpaid lunch

Employees tap **Start lunch** in the clock bar while clocked in (up to 3 per shift, `LUNCH_MAX_MINUTES`, default 90). The clock and job-site checks pause and the phone stops reading GPS. **End lunch** needs a fresh precise fix inside the job-site radius, otherwise they can still clock out. Lunch minutes are subtracted from paid time and noted on the time entry; if nobody returns in time the shift is closed at the moment lunch began and the office is alerted. Admins see "on lunch" in Clocked in now and lunch events on the shift timeline.

## Live employee board

Admin → **Live now** shows every active employee: clocked in or not, on site / off site / signal lost / on lunch, the job they are on, time today, last GPS fix (map link), device, the latest job update and recent location events. It refreshes every 20 seconds while open and lets an admin clock someone out. API: `GET /api/admin/location/live`.

## Hiring skills test

After applying, each applicant is sent to (and emailed a link for) `/handyman-application/test/?token=...`. They take a 15-question multiple-choice test for each trade they selected (`lib/skill-tests.js`: Tile setter, Tile helper, Remodeler / carpenter, Plumber, Electrician, Painter / finisher, Drywall, General handyman; "Other" uses General handyman). Questions and options are shuffled per attempt from a server-side seed and answers never leave the server. Pass mark is 70% (11/15), the time limit is 30 minutes (late submissions are flagged), and there is one attempt per trade. Admin → HR → Applications shows scores, missed questions and a "Reset attempt" button. The questions are general screeners; have a lead tradesperson review them before relying on scores.

## Permit tracking

Each estimate has a **Permits and inspections** card (admin edits, estimators view): permit type, city, status (needed, applied, issued, inspections, final approved, not required), number, fee, dates, customer and internal notes, and inspections with results. ## Tool photos

Admin → **Tool photos** lists every tool and supply on the crew checklist (`lib/tools.js`). For each one the admin uploads a JPG, PNG or WebP (3 MB max) with a credit and optional source address; quick links open Home Depot, Google Images and Wikimedia Commons searches to find one. The photos appear as thumbnails next to each item on the estimator's "Tools needed" list and the employee portal's "Tools checklist" (tap to enlarge). Photos are only served to admins and active employees. Only upload photos you took or have the right to use. The app cannot download photos itself because the server has no outside access, so the library starts empty.

## Onboarding and training

New employees (created in Admin → Employees) sign in with the emailed setup link and see only the onboarding wizard (`assets/onboard.js`, `lib/onboarding.js`): personal and emergency details, W-4 withholding and I-9 section 1 answers with a typed e-signature, direct deposit (bank name and last 4 only), the employment agreement and location-tracking consent, then an experience profile (trades, 20 task comfort ratings, certifications, tools, vehicle). Each signature stores the typed name, IP, device, time and a SHA-256 of the exact text. Training (`lib/training.js`) has six lesson modules with quizzes (80% to pass) and a 10-question final exam (3 attempts); the admin sets 1-3 days (a new day unlocks about every 18 hours) and signs the employee off, which unlocks clock-in, jobs and field work (`employees.onboarding` is the stage; empty means onboarded, as for everyone hired earlier or hired with "Skip"). Quiz answers stay on the server and are shuffled per employee. Admin → **Onboarding** lists progress, finds people by task or trade experience, and edits the agreement text; each employee file shows signed documents, tax answers, training scores and experience.


## Tool and supply photos

Every tool and supply already has a built-in, detailed vector illustration (`lib/tool-art.js`, no API or key needed). It is served automatically until an admin uploads a real photo, which then replaces it.

`AI_API_KEY=... node scripts/generate-tool-images.js` generates a realistic product photo for every tool and supply in the crew catalog (189 items) and stores it in the tool photo library. It skips tools that already have a photo (`--force` replaces them, `--only key,key` limits it, `--data-dir DIR` selects the data folder). Uses `AI_BASE_URL` and `AI_IMAGE_MODEL` (default `gpt-image-1`). Re-run to retry any failures.

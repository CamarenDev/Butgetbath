# Butgetbath
Lead-generation website for Budget Bath Solutions (bathroom remodeling and tile in Kane, Kendall, DuPage and DeKalb counties, Illinois).

- Static, SEO-focused site generated by `build.js` (Bootstrap 5.3), with homeowner help, community Q&A, service-area and legal pages.
- Free customer accounts: email verification, photo and video uploads (photos up to 8 MB, MP4/MOV/WebM videos up to 100 MB, 10 files per request) with per-file comments, and a threaded request (ticket) system with categories, priorities, a progress stepper, upcoming appointments, a staff inbox (search, sort, filters), internal notes, email notifications and email replies. Email is sent with Resend.
- Landing page built for booking: an above-the-fold estimate form (preferred day and time), FAQ with schema markup and local business schema.

```
npm install
npm start    # builds and serves on http://localhost:3000
npm test
```

Configuration, Resend setup and deployment notes are in [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md). Copy `.env.example` to `.env` and never commit it.

## Appointments

Staff (`ADMIN_EMAILS`) schedule, reschedule and cancel appointments from a customer's request page. Each change emails the customer a confirmation with an `.ics` invite (`METHOD:REQUEST`/`CANCEL`), plus "Add to Google Calendar" and Apple/Outlook links. Customers see upcoming appointments on their dashboard and can cancel (staff are emailed).

- **iCalendar**: per-appointment download at `/api/appointments/:id/ics`, and a private subscription feed for staff (shown on the staff requests page) that works with Apple Calendar, Outlook and Google Calendar "From URL".
- **Google Calendar sync** (optional): set `GOOGLE_CALENDAR_ID` and `GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON` (see `.env.example`). Without them, only the links, `.ics` files and feed are used.

## Bathroom estimator
- `/estimator/` (staff, mobile-first): quick estimator, price book (parts, labor, equipment, subs), markup, contingency, tax on materials, deposits, optional add-ons, change orders, payments and legal documents. Admins can allow individual employees from their employee page; employees see only their own drafts and never markup or profit.
- Customers review, sign and pay from `/account/estimate/?id=N`.
- **Smart takeoff (`lib/bathroom.js`):** about 250 price-book items (fans, trim, flooring, paint, toilets and internals, plumbing, electrical, vanities, hardware, shower doors, glass, accessories, permits). The wizard adapts to bathroom type, size, ceiling height, home age (lead-safe, asbestos test, old supply and cast-iron drains), floor level, hidden-damage condition, flooring, shower build, glass, vanity and toilet choices, and lists suggested upgrades as optional lines. `POST /api/estimator/advice` previews notes and price live.
- **Full remodel mode:** the `fullRemodel` option guts walls and ceiling to the studs and joists and the floor to the subfloor, then adds stud inspection, full plumbing and electrical rough-in, insulation, drywall, extra haul-away and cleanup. **Results PDF:** `POST /api/estimator/advice/pdf` (staff) downloads the wizard results (notes, sections, optional upgrades, totals) from the New estimate page.
- **AI learning (`lib/learning.js`):** after a job, an admin records the real cost on the signed estimate ("Teach the AI estimator"; `POST /api/estimator/estimates/:id/outcome`). The estimator compares it with its uncalibrated prediction and learns a correction per section and from the most similar past jobs (recency-weighted, shrunk toward 1 until there is evidence, capped at -25%/+40%, so corrections never compound). Admin → **AI learning** shows what it learned and its accuracy. New estimates also get a recommended contingency (older home, poor condition, full gut) and a trade-by-trade schedule with working days.
- **Photo estimating (`lib/vision.js`):** the New estimate page can read 1 to 4 bathroom photos with a vision AI model, fill in the questions and list risks and assumptions. It is off until `AI_API_KEY` is set (optional `AI_MODEL`, default `gpt-4.1`, and `AI_BASE_URL` for any OpenAI-compatible API). Staff must confirm customer consent, photos are sent only for analysis and never stored, and only whitelisted answers are accepted. Treat results as a starting point and measure on site.
- **Crew, hours and work calendar:** every estimate gets a phase-by-phase plan with crew size, labor hours, working days and dates. Admin → Defaults sets the work day (start/end), unpaid lunch, paid breaks and whether Saturdays are worked (Sundays are always off). Productive hours per person = shift minus lunch and breaks; shorter productive days stretch the schedule, waiting phases (inspection, cure, lab) run on calendar days, and an optional planned start date drives the dates. Real job hours recorded under "Teach the AI estimator" correct future hour estimates.
- **Tools checklist:** every estimate produces a list of the tools, rentals, safety gear and supplies the job needs, built from what the estimate includes (a gut remodel adds demolition, rough-in and respirator gear; an LVP floor swaps in LVP tools). It appears on the estimate editor and the results PDF. When the job is created from a signed estimate it is copied to the employee portal, where assigned employees tick items off. Admin can refresh the crew list from the estimate. The list is built into `lib/tools.js` from standard trade practice, not from live web research; edit that file to change it.
- **Estimator insights (35 checks, `lib/insights.js`):** confidence score and price range, good/better/best, risk register with dollar exposure, margin, price per sq ft benchmark, sensitivity, price age, quote validity, shopping list, lead times, payment milestones, value engineering, aging in place, code checks, fan sizing, circuit load, tile order, dumpster size, trade hours, downtime, homeowner prep, warranty, resale, cost drivers, missing-input prompts, plain-English summary, inspections, seasonal note, premiums, allowances, learning status, cost by kind and cost per day. Admin → Defaults adds a local labor rate factor and Saturday premium. They appear under the wizard and in the results PDF. Benchmarks are reference values, not live data.
- **Permits and codes (`lib/codes.js`):** enter the job's city in the wizard and the estimator lists the Illinois statewide rules, which permits the scope likely needs (building, plumbing, electrical), the inspections to book, lead/asbestos/condo flags and city notes. It appears in the wizard and the results PDF. Illinois has no single statewide building code and each city adopts its own, so city entries are starter data marked unverified until an admin confirms them with the building department and saves them with `POST /api/estimator/codes`. It is guidance, not legal advice, and does not cover every Illinois municipality.
- **Handyman jobs (`lib/handyman.js`):** choose "Handyman / home repair" in New estimate and pick from about 140 tasks in 14 categories (drywall, carpentry, doors and windows, flooring, kitchen, plumbing, electrical, smart home, exterior, deck and fence, painting, assembly, safety and HVAC, haul-away). Each task has hours and a Home Depot reference material price. The planner adds labor at the handyman or licensed rate, a minimum service visit, permit allowances, a crew schedule, permits and inspections, lead/asbestos flags, insights and a task-based tool checklist. Admins edit task prices under Handyman tasks and rates under Defaults. Prices are reference values, not live. Handyman jobs do not feed the bathroom AI learning.
- **Downtown Chicago high-rise SEO (`build.js`):** a `/chicago-high-rise-remodeling/` hub plus pages for the Loop, River North, Streeterville, South Loop, West Loop, Gold Coast and New Eastside/Lakeshore East. Each has FAQ and Service structured data, appears in the sitemap, the nav, the footer and the home page, and Chicago is added to the business `areaServed`. Add a neighborhood by editing `hoods` in `build.js`.
- **Local prices and tool rental:** defaults are Home Depot reference prices for Kane, Kendall, DuPage and DeKalb counties (Midwest), dated in `prices_updated`. A "Tool rental" section sizes rentals (tile saw, demo hammer, mixer, air scrubber, scaffold, dolly, delivery) to the job. Items marked "(est.)" are typical local rental-yard rates. Prices cannot auto-refresh from Home Depot, so update them in the Price book; new seed items are added to existing databases at startup without overwriting edits.
- **PDFs and e-signatures:** estimates and legal documents download as PDFs (`/api/account/estimates/:id/pdf`, `/api/estimator/estimates/:id/pdf`). Typed-name signing records the name, UTC time, IP address, device, ESIGN/UETA consent text and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the signed content; the PDF prints this block, and a signed copy is emailed to the customer and `NOTIFY_EMAIL`. Admins can look up a fingerprint at `GET /api/estimator/verify?hash=...`. Signed estimates are locked; use a change order. Legal templates are generic starting points; have an attorney review them.
